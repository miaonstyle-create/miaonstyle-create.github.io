Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Mia On Style Studio ("the App") is an internal tool operated by Mia On Style ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is used.

1. Whose data we access

The App only connects to social media accounts owned by Mia On Style. We do not offer the App to other users, and we do not log in to or collect data from accounts we do not own.

2. Data we access

With the account owner's authorization, the App may access:

  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID), the list of our own videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares), and permission to upload and publish videos to our account.
  • Instagram, Facebook and Threads: our own profile and Page information, our own posts and their insights, and comments and messages sent to our own accounts.

3. How we use the data

  • to publish our own content;
  • to measure the performance of our posts and create internal reports;
  • to read and reply to comments and messages on our own accounts.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising profiling.
  • We do not access personal data beyond what is needed for the purposes above.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens and reports are stored locally on the Mia On Style team's computer and protected by the operating system's security. Data is kept only as long as needed for reporting, and is deleted when no longer needed.

6. Comments and messages from other users

When people comment on or message our accounts, the App may read the text of those comments or messages so that we can reply or moderate them. This content is used only for that purpose and is not shared.

7. Revoking access and deletion requests

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's app permission settings. To request deletion of any data related to you, email namhoangcoaching@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
